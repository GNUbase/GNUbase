surround-lfe.ogg is a generated one-second, six-channel signal at 48 kHz with a 1 kHz sine of amplitude 0.25 in
LFE only. It checks the Vorbis source order, where LFE is channel six, independently of WAVE's channel order.
Generated with FFmpeg and its libvorbis encoder, without source recordings:

ffmpeg -f lavfi -i 'aevalsrc=0|0|0|0.25*sin(2*PI*1000*t)|0|0:s=48000:d=1:c=5.1' -c:a libvorbis -q:a 4 -threads 1 \
  -map_metadata -1 surround-lfe.ogg
