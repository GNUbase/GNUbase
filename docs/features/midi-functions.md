# MIDI functions

Functions that edit MIDI parts as a whole (#133, #838).

- **MIDI functions** (`src/engine/midi/functions.h`, #133) edit parts as a whole: `midi.transpose` (semitones, scale correction from one scale to another, keeping notes in a range), `midi.legato`, `midi.fixLengths`, `midi.fixVelocities`, `midi.velocity` (add, compress or expand, limit, fixed, variance; reference manual pp. 1126-1127), `midi.pedalToNoteLength`, `midi.deleteOverlaps` (mono and poly), `midi.deleteDoubles`, `midi.deleteControllers`, `midi.deleteContinuousControllers`, `midi.deleteNotes`, `midi.restrictPolyphony`, `midi.thinOut`, `midi.reverse` and `midi.invert`. Each takes `parts` and, where notes matter, the selected `notes` (all of them if left out), since the selection is not in the document. Notes on different MIDI channels are separate voices: they never shorten, delete or limit each other. Variance lowers each note by a random part that comes from the ids of its part and itself, so it is the same every time. The functions are plain functions on the document; `functioncommands.cpp` registers them through `ProjectModel::addEdit`, so each is one undo step and can be part of a transaction.
- **Functions that make parts.** `midi.merge` copies what starts between two positions (in samples, like the locators) on some tracks into a new part, leaving out muted parts (p. 1119). Without `tracks` it takes the MIDI and instrument tracks that play, which `playingTracks` finds by playback's rules: it compiles a render snapshot, and a track plays if neither its channel nor a channel its output leads into is silent there (muted, silenced by another channel's solo, or on a disabled track); with none, it refuses. `midi.dissolve` copies events by channel or pitch into new parts at the same place and mutes the parts (pp. 1121-1122): `to: tracks` puts them on new MIDI tracks routed to the same instrument, `to: lanes` on new lanes of the track below the lanes its parts are on (`optimize` only works with tracks). Events of no channel or pitch go with the first new part of their part, so the new parts play what the part did. `midi.bounce` combines a track's parts into one on the lowest of their lanes, removes muted parts and writes the parts' transpose and velocity into the notes (p. 1123), as playback applies them: a note transposed beyond 0-127, which doesn't play, is left out.
- **Extract MIDI Automation** (`midi.extractAutomation`, p. 1129) moves the continuous controllers of parts into controller automation lanes of their tracks (`automationLane.*`, [automation.md](automation.md)), one per controller and channel: a step point where each event plays, its value scaled to 0-1. An existing lane loses its points in the part's range first. Pitch bend, pressure, switches such as the sustain pedal, events past a part's end and muted parts stay, as on p. 1129.
- **The MIDI menu** (`resources/ui/project.rml`, `src/app/appmidifunctions.cpp`, reference manual pp. 1118-1131) runs them in the app. As in the reference, they work on what the Edit menu's quantize commands take ([quantize.md](quantize.md)): in an editor, its selected notes, or every note it edits without a selection; from no zone (Lua, MCP, the menu in the project window), an open editor's selected notes first, else the project window's selected MIDI parts. MIDI > Functions: Legato (`midi.functionsLegato`) extends by the user setting `editing.legatoOverlap` (Legato Overlap, in ticks of 480 to a quarter note, negative for a gap) and, with `editing.legatoBetweenSelected` (Legato Mode: Between Selected Notes Only, p. 1599), only to the next selected note; Fixed Lengths sets the editor's length quantize, or the quantize grid's step with its tuplet when that is linked to it, as in the project window and as Edit > Quantize Lengths takes it (swing and grooves, which change from step to step, don't count); Fixed Velocity the editor's insert velocity (100 in the project window); Pedals to Note Length, Delete Overlaps (mono) and (poly), Delete Doubles, Delete Controllers, Delete Continuous Controllers, Thin Out Data, Extract MIDI Automation, Reverse and Mirror (`midi.invert`) run with no settings; Velocity... (Type with Amount, Ratio in percent, Lower and Upper, or Variance in percent; velocities in MIDI 1.0 values), Delete Notes... (Minimum Length in ticks of 480 to a quarter note, Minimum Velocity, Remove when under with both; p. 1140) and Restrict Polyphony... ask first. Transpose Setup... asks for the semitones, Scale Correction from a current to a new scale and Keep Notes in Range with the highest and lowest note. Merge MIDI in Loop... (MIDI Merge Options: Erase Destination, Include Chase) merges between the locators the tracks that play onto the first selected MIDI or instrument track, or a new one. Dissolve Part... separates the project window's selected parts by channel or by pitch, onto tracks with Optimized Display or onto lanes; Bounce MIDI bounces the selected parts of each track, one undo step for several tracks. The dialogs are one document, `resources/ui/midi-function.rml`, whose Lua runs the engine's command and shows its error if it refuses, and it enables the fields the chosen settings use.
- **One dialog at a time.** Dialogs whose Lua keeps its state in a global table open with `AppUi::openOnce`, which takes the document's attributes: while one is open, opening it again, from another window or through MCP, brings it to the front as it is. The MIDI menu's dialogs, Add Track and Auto Fades work this way; the fade and crossfade editors already open one at a time through `EventDisplay::openFadeDialog`.
- **Not done yet:** MIDI effects in merge (Include Inserts with the track's MIDI modifiers, which #778 takes up with the parts' transpose and velocity in merge, Include Sends, Convert VST3) and chase options beyond controllers; giving the tracks that Dissolve Part makes the MIDI channel they hold (MIDI tracks have no channel setting yet); note names in Transpose Setup's range fields; Calculate Tempo from MIDI Events comes with #158.

## Checks

`tests/midifunctions_tests.cpp` has a test for each function, including `playingTracks` with solo, mute, a muted group channel and a disabled track. `tests/midifunctions_ui_tests.cpp` drives the menu with `ui::Automation`: every item of MIDI > Functions without a dialog, once each, run on a part through the menu and the engine's command with the expected settings on a copy of it on another track, comparing notes, controllers and automation, so that a wrong command or mode fails; the item list is checked against the submenu, and no two items may give the same result. It also runs Legato with and without an overlap and between selected notes, Fixed Lengths with a tuplet grid, Fixed Velocity, Reverse and Mirror on the project window's selected parts and on an editor's selected notes, and the dialogs: Transpose Setup by semitones, with scale correction, keeping notes in range and with a value the engine refuses, Velocity (Add/Subtract and Limit), Delete Notes, Restrict Polyphony, Dissolve Part by channel, by pitch and to lanes, Merge MIDI in Loop onto a new track, refused with no track playing and onto the selected one with Erase Destination, Bounce MIDI, and that a second dialog brings the open one to the front.
