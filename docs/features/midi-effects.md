# MIDI effects

The MIDI insert and send slots of MIDI and instrument tracks, and GNUbase's built-in MIDI effects that play in them (#135, reference manual pp. 1104–1106). They change the track's events as it plays them and add new ones; the notes in the document stay as they are. The slots are the channel's `midiInserts` and `midiSends` in `channel.set` and `project.get`, so also the MCP tool `channel_set` and `gnubase.channel.set` in Lua, and they are saved in GNUbase's entry of the project file. `midiEffect.list` lists the effects with their parameters.

- **The slots** (`midi::EffectRack` in `src/engine/midi/effects.h`): each section has four slots and `bypass`, which bypasses all of them; the sends also have `pre`. A slot has an `effect` (an id from `midiEffect.list`, or empty for none), `on` and the effect's `parameters`, by key (`mode`, `rate`, ...), each a number or the name of one of its options (`"Up/Down"`, `"1/16"`); `project.get` shows numbers. `channel.set` changes only what it is given, also within each slot (`slots: [{}, {"on": false}]` switches the second one off); a slot that gets another effect starts from that effect's defaults.
- **How they play:** the track's own events, those of its parts (after the MIDI modifiers, [midi-modifiers.md](midi-modifiers.md)) and those played into it (`midi.inject`, MIDI thru), go through the inserts in order, each effect playing what the one before it played. Each send plays its effect on the same notes (note-ons, note-offs and poly pressure), after the inserts or, with `pre`, before them, and what it plays goes to the track's output beside what the inserts play: the unprocessed events and the effect's together, as on p. 1106. The sends get no other events, so the track's controllers, pitch bend, program changes and SysEx reach the output once, through the inserts. A MIDI track routed to an instrument track comes with its own effects; the instrument track's effects play its own events only, as with the modifiers. A MIDI track routed away from an instrument ends the notes its effects played there too: `MidiSequencer::holds` counts them with its own. They play while the transport stands too.
- **The effects** (`src/engine/processors/midieffects.h`) are built-in processors, `processors::MidiEffect`s: they handle events at their sample offsets and leave audio alone, take their parameters as parameter events or from the track's sequencer, and end the notes they started when the notes that made them end, so a stop or a jump, where the sequencer ends its notes, ends theirs too. When their output is full, note-offs take the places of other events, so no note hangs; the arpeggiator ends its note in the next block instead.
  - **Arpeggiator:** plays the held notes one after another, a step per note value of the tempo (`rate` 1/4, 1/8, 1/16 or 1/32), from the first note-on on: `mode` up, down, up and down (the top and bottom notes once each turn) or in the order played, over one to four `octaves`, each note as long as `gate` per cent of a step and as loud as the note it comes from. Up to 16 notes are held at a time. A step whose note-on finds no room in a full output is left out, and so are the steps whose time has passed by the next block: they don't play late.
  - **Chord generator:** plays a chord for every note, the note its root: major, minor, diminished, augmented, sus2, sus4, major 7, minor 7, 7, power chord or octave. Where two chords share a pitch it plays once and ends with the last of them; a note's note-off ends the chord its note-on started, also after the chord was changed.
  - **Transformer**, a transformer-style effect: the notes that meet its `condition` (all notes, or those whose pitch, MIDI 1.0 velocity or channel, 1 to 16, lies from `minimum` to `maximum`) are transposed by `value` (beyond 0 to 127 they are deleted), get it as their velocity or have it added (velocities stay from 1 to 127), move to channel `value` (1 to 16) or are deleted. A note-off and poly pressure go where their note-on went. Where two notes end up on one pitch and channel (two channels set to one, or a note transposed onto one that passes), it plays once and ends with the last of them, as in the chord generator. It is a rule of the rule editor's kind with a fixed shape and plays through the input transformers' `LogicalStage` ([midi-rules.md](midi-rules.md)).
- **Real time and determinism:** the sequencer makes the effects on the command thread when the render snapshot changes, keeping the instance of an effect that stays in its slot so its state goes on, and runs them on the audio thread on buffers it allocated beforehand. An effect that stops playing (removed, replaced, switched off or bypassed) loses its state. When the effects that play change, the sequencer ends every note it sounds at the next block, those the effects played too, so none hangs; the notes held go on without sounding until they are played again. The arpeggiator counts its steps in frames from its first note and takes each step's length from the tempo where the step starts (the project's tempo map in the transport block, `TransportBlock::tempoMap`), so an arpeggio comes out the same for every block size (rule 6), also under tempo changes and ramps.

## Not yet

The inspector's MIDI Inserts and MIDI Sends sections and the effects' control panels (pp. 1104–1106); the divider, which records what the inserts above it play (p. 1105); sends to another MIDI output than the track's (p. 1106); sends before the MIDI modifiers for the parts' notes (`pre` takes the events before the inserts, after the modifiers: the render snapshot holds the parts' notes with the modifiers applied); automating the effects' parameters; the effects' events through the plugin API (`BuiltinPlugin` leaves them out of `builtin:` plugins), all in #992; more effects and an arpeggiator that follows the bars instead of its first note.

## Checks

`tests/midieffects_tests.cpp`: golden MIDI output (`tests/golden/midi-effect-*.txt`, what `midi.capture` returned, a line per message) of the arpeggiator in each mode, the chord generator with shared pitches and a chord changed while a note sounds, the transformer with each condition and action, and inserts in order with a send before and after them on a part's notes; slots that are off, a bypassed section and empty slots; an arpeggio that is the same for every block size, also under a tempo ramp; notes ended when the effects change; a MIDI track's effects on its way to an instrument track, and the notes they played there ended when it is routed away; the arpeggiator with a full output, which ends its note once there is room and leaves out the steps it missed; note-offs of the chord generator and the transformer in a full output; two notes the transformer puts on one pitch; sends that repeat no controllers, pitch bend or pressure; `channel.set`, `project.get` and undo, what it refuses, saving and loading; `midiEffect.list`. `tests/rtcheck_tests.cpp` runs every effect and a sequencer with effects without allocating.
