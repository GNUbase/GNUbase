# Sending and receiving MIDI

How tests, scripts and AI agents play MIDI into GNUbase and read back what its instruments played (#29), through the engine commands `midi.inject` and `midi.capture`, so also as the MCP tools `midi_inject` and `midi_capture` and as `gnubase.midi.inject` and `gnubase.midi.capture` in Lua.

- **Sending:** `midi.inject` plays MIDI 1.0 channel messages (a status byte from 0x80 to 0xEF and its data bytes; no system messages or SysEx) into the channel of a MIDI or instrument track, as if they came from the track's MIDI input. The track sends them on to its instrument, a MIDI track to the instrument track it is routed to, after the events it receives and before the events of its parts. It does that also while the transport stands, as when playing a keyboard. A note-on of velocity 0 is a note-off of velocity 64.
- **Timing:** each message has a `position`, its frame on the audio device, counted as `audio.capture`, `midi.monitor` and `audio.get` count them. The dummy device renders offline (`audio.render`), so a message plays at exactly its frame, for any block size. Messages for frames the device has processed already play at the start of its next block; messages for one frame play in the order they came. Up to 4096 wait for the audio thread at a time; when the device changes (`audio.setDevice`, `audio.reset`), the waiting ones are dropped, since the new device starts at position 0.
- **Receiving:** `midi.capture` returns what instruments played since the last call, oldest first, at most 4096 messages: each with its instrument track's channel, its frame position on the audio device and its MIDI 1.0 bytes. That is everything the instrument gets: the events of the track's parts and of the MIDI tracks routed to it, what was injected, MIDI controller automation and the note-offs at a stop. A program change with a bank comes as bank select MSB (controller 0) and LSB (controller 32), then the program change. SysEx isn't captured yet. `dropped` counts the messages that didn't fit.
- **A round trip** (`tests/mcptools_tests.cpp`, `tests/lua/midi.lua`): inject notes into an instrument track playing the test synth and into a MIDI track routed to it, render, and get back the notes with their frames from `midi.capture` and the audio from `audio.capture` or `audio.analyze`.

## How it works

- `MidiTap` (`src/engine/audio/miditap.h`) keeps two lock-free queues. The command thread pushes injected messages into one; at the start of each block the audio thread moves them into a list it owns and gives each sequencer the messages due in the block, as events at their offsets (`MidiSequencer::input`). Every MIDI and instrument channel has a sequencer, which lasts across graph rebuilds; each program lists them by channel id. A message for a channel that is gone is dropped; one for a sequencer whose input is full plays in the next block.
- After the graph processed the block, the audio thread converts the events each instrument channel gave its instrument (`InstrumentChannel::played`) to MIDI 1.0 and pushes them into the other queue, which `midi.capture` empties. No allocations, locks or I/O on the audio thread.
- Not here: MIDI from the MIDI input ports reaching tracks, with track inputs, MIDI thru and recording, comes with MIDI recording (#97); `midi.monitor` shows that input until then. MIDI output to ports comes with MIDI tracks that play external instruments.
