# Channel formats

Speaker arrangements of busses and channels, and the conversion between them (#269).

- **Formats.** Every bus and audio channel has a format (`ChannelFormat` in `src/engine/document/channelformat.h`): a speaker arrangement with a fixed speaker order, not just a channel count. GNUbase has mono, stereo and the reference's 2D surround formats: `LRC`, `LRS`, `LRC+LFE`, `LRS+LFE`, `LRCS`, `LRCS+LFE`, `quadro`, `quadro+LFE`, `5.0`, `5.1`, `6.0 cine`, `6.0 music`, `7.0` and `7.1`. The speakers follow Microsoft's order, as in the reference and in WAVE files: the front channels, then LFE, the surround channels and the side channels, so 5.1 is L R C LFE Ls Rs and 7.1 is L R C LFE Ls Rs Sl Sr. MIDI channels have the format `none`. The 3D formats come with the surround panner (#188), Ambisonics with #190.
- **Commands.** `bus.add`, `track.add` and `channel.set` take a `format` by name, or `channels` (1 to 8), which picks mono, stereo, LRC, quadro, 5.0, 5.1, 7.0 or 7.1; given both, they must agree. Busses and channels report both their `format` and their `channels`, and `graph.get` shows each node's format. A new format is one undo step and is announced with `channel.changed`.
- **Conversion.** Where a channel feeds a destination of another format (a bus, a group channel; an input bus feeding a track), the graph converts the audio while it sums its inputs (`conversionGains`, applied in `CompiledGraph`): speakers that both formats have connect one to one, the others fold into the destination's speakers. The levels are the reference's automatic downmix tables, and other combinations chain through them:
  - 7.1 to 5.1: Sl into L and Ls, Sr into R and Rs, at -3.01 dB each.
  - 5.1 to stereo: C and LFE into L and R at -3.01 dB, Ls into L and Rs into R at -6.02 dB. So 7.1 to stereo is 7.1 to 5.1 to stereo.
  - A surround center (Cs, the S of LRCS and LRS) splits into Ls and Rs at -3.01 dB, and Ls and Rs merge into it at -3.01 dB where the destination has a surround center but no Ls and Rs.
  - L and R merge into mono at -3.01 dB each, so mono to stereo and back keeps its level. Mono goes to C, or to L and R at the pan law's center (-3 dB) where there is no C.
  - Narrower formats feed wider ones speaker by speaker: stereo reaches L and R of 5.1, which has nothing on its other speakers. The surround panner (#188) will place mono and stereo channels in wider formats.
- The gains are worked out when the graph compiles, off the audio thread; summing a node's inputs then multiplies and adds in a fixed order, so rendering stays bit-identical for any number of threads. A connection between equal formats adds without multiplying, as before.
- **DAWproject** stores only a channel count (`audioChannels`), so the format is saved in GNUbase's extra entry. A project read from `project.xml` alone gets the default format for each count: 6 channels become 5.1, more than 8 become 7.1. Clips keep playing on the track's channels one to one, a mono clip on every channel (`TrackPlayer`); converting a clip's format is not part of this.
