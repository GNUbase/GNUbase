# Metronome and count-in

The click that follows the project's tempo and time signatures, pickup bars included, and the bars it counts in before a recording starts (#101, reference manual pp. 319-329). The code is in `src/engine/metronome`.

## Settings
- The settings belong to the project: they are saved with it (the `metronome` object of GNUbase's extra DAWproject entry) but changing them is no undo step. `metronome.get` returns them, `metronome.set` changes the fields it is given and leaves the others, `metronome.setPattern` sets or removes the click pattern of one time signature. Each change emits `metronome.changed` with all settings.
- **The metronome switch** is the transport bar's (`transport.set` `metronome`, key C): it turns the click on. `whilePlaying` and `whileRecording` say when the click sounds then, segment by segment, so it follows punching in and out at the exact sample. A mixdown leaves the switch off.
- **Audio click** (`audio`): played through one or more output busses (`outputs`, the main mix if empty or if the chosen busses are gone) at `level` dB. The sounds are `beeps` (frequency and level per accent) or `samples` (an audio file and level per accent). A sample that can't be read plays its accent's beep instead; `metronome.set` rejects a new sample path that can't be read. Samples are decoded to mono, cut at 10 seconds and converted to the device's sample rate on the command thread, and decoded again at the next change to the project or the device after the file changed.
- **MIDI click** (`midi`): a note per accent (`notes`, with velocity) on MIDI `channel` 1–16, sent to the instrument channel `destination`. Sending it to a MIDI output port comes with MIDI output routing.
- **Accents and patterns**: there are four accents, 1 the strongest. A bar of a signature without its own pattern clicks once per beat of the signature, accent 1 on the first. A pattern (`patterns`, one per signature with a numerator up to 64) divides the bar into up to 64 equal clicks and gives each an accent 1–4 or 0 for silence, e.g. 6/8 as two clicks per bar or 7/8 as 2+2+3.
- **Count-in** (`countIn`): with `enabled`, `transport.record` from stop counts in `bars` 1–16 first, whether the switch is on or not; playing, and recording while playing, don't count in. It ends where playback starts, the pre-roll's start if there is one. Its `signature` is `start` (the signature and tempo where recording starts), `project` (the bars before where playback starts, as the project has them, with their tempo changes; it begins `bars` bars before the bar playback starts in and runs up to the start, so from a start within a bar it also counts that bar's first beats) or `custom` (`customSignature` at the tempo where recording starts).

Patterns per signature event, as opposed to per signature, and the setup dialog come later.

## Processing
- The metronome is a built-in processor (`Metronome`), added to the graph as its last node, so that the switch turns the click on without a rebuild. Its mono output goes to the chosen output busses, its events to the destination instrument channel, so busses sum it in their fixed order like every other input.
- The command thread compiles the settings into a `ClickConfig` (sounds with their level, patterns, tempo and signature maps) and publishes it to the audio thread; it compiles again only when the settings, the tempo or signature map, a sample rate or a sample file changed.
- `clicksIn` (`clicks.h`) lists the clicks within a range of samples: bar by bar, each bar divided into its pattern's clicks at exact tick positions, converted to samples with the same rounding as the tempo map. The clicks of a block don't depend on where blocks start, so the click is the same for every block size and thread count.
- Eight voices play the audio click; a new click takes a free voice or the one that started first. The voices are rendered one after another into the output, so the sum is the same every time. A MIDI click note lasts 50 ms; a new click ends the note before.

## Count-in in the transport
- `transport.record` from stop hands the transport a `graph::CountIn`: its length in samples, and how its clicks are placed (on the project timeline before the start, or in one tempo and signature). The transport then counts in instead of playing: `TransportBlock` carries the count-in frames and its position, and the metronome clicks them. Playback and recording start at the exact sample the count-in ends, within the block, as `transport.record` from stop starts them. `TransportBlock::click` carries the switch.
- Meanwhile `transport.get` reports `countingIn` and playing, and recording if recording starts where playback starts: it doesn't change when the count-in ends, only when a pre-roll after it reaches the record start. The audio thread publishes counting in as a third state bit, which the command thread predicts as it does playing and recording.
- Stopping, `transport.record` false or toggling record ends the count-in at once and leaves the cursor where it was; Lock Record doesn't keep it from stopping, as nothing is recorded yet. A locate, or new transport settings that move where playback starts (a pre-roll, the start mode), start the count-in again from there, so it still ends on that bar line. A mixdown waits until it has ended, like a recording.
- While counting in, the playback streams are cued where playback will start, as for a stopped transport (`Transport::countInStart`), so audio plays from the first sample after the count-in, pre-roll included.
- Frames of a block before playback starts are stopped for plugins (a stopped transport segment first) and `-1` for the `positionProbe`.
